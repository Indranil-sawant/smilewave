Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we handle information submitted on this website.

This Privacy Policy describes how SmileWave™ Dental Clinic collects and processes information submitted through our website forms. We do not sell or lease patient-identifiable data to third-party advertisers.

Information We Collect

When you submit an appointment enquiry on this website, you provide details such as your full name, phone number, email address, patient type, requested service, and preferred date/time slots. This information is purely used to coordinate booking logistics.

Cookies and Tracking

This website is static and does not deploy invasive tracking cookies. We may use simple browser cookies to support basic interface states (like retaining navigation styles) or local security validations.

Data Retention & Security

Your query submissions are treated with high clinical confidentiality. Access to submitted contact information is restricted to authorized clinic front-desk coordinators. We retain submitted data only as long as necessary to fulfill appointment coordination requests.

User Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the contact information you submitted through our website forms. Please write or call our clinic desk at +91 97693 11848 to initiate any request.
