Privacy Policy

SmileWave collects only the information needed to schedule and deliver dental care. Appointment requests may include name, phone number, preferred visit time, and notes you choose to share.

We do not sell personal data. Information sent through WhatsApp or email is used by the clinic team to confirm bookings and follow up on treatment. You may ask us to update or delete contact details by calling or writing to hello@smilewave.com.

This static website does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated.
