PATIENT GUIDANCE

Common Dental Concerns We Help With

Tap on any concern below to understand standard clinical causes and next steps.

Toothache

Tooth pain may indicate active decay, a crack, or pulp inflammation. An exam and X-ray help locate the issue.

Tooth Sensitivity

Pain with cold or hot substances often stems from exposed dentin or worn enamel. Sealants or fillings can shield the tooth surface.

Cavities

Decaying spots in teeth need early intervention. Direct composite fillings stop cavities from reaching internal tooth nerves.

Broken/Damaged Teeth

Chipped or fractured structure is repaired using direct bonding veneers or high-strength porcelain crowns to restore chewing shape.

Missing Teeth

Gaps can cause adjacent teeth to shift. Long-term replacement options include bone-anchored dental implants or bridges.

Gum Concerns

Bleeding gums or swelling indicates mild to severe periodontal infection. Professional deep scaling cleans plaque buildup.

Children's Dental Problems

Early cavities or dental anxiety in children are resolved using gentle tell-show-do child dentistry methods.

Smile Concerns

Spacing or teeth discoloration can be discussed via custom cosmetic smile-enhancement options.

Routine Dental Care

6-monthly check-ups support prevention. Cleaning removes calculus buildup that regular brushing cannot reach.

TOOTH PRESERVATION

When a Tooth Needs More Than a Filling

Root canal treatment may be recommended when the inner tissue (pulp) of a tooth becomes infected or significantly inflamed due to decay or injury. Treatment is intended to remove the affected tissue and help preserve your natural tooth.

Clinical Treatment Journey:

  1. Diagnostic clinical check & low-dose X-ray examination.
  2. Pulp infection cleanup and root disinfection.
  3. Hermetic root sealing to protect against bacterial entry.
  4. Coronal restoration using composite sealants or crowns.

TOOTH REPLACEMENT

Replacing Missing Teeth

Dental implants serve as a stable, bone-anchored replacement option for individual missing teeth. Titanium posts function as replacement roots to support single or multiple custom crowns securely.

What to Expect: Suitability depends on an individual clinical check of jawbone density and overall systemic health.

Clinical Planning: High-resolution diagnostics and detailed models shape placement timelines explicitly before surgery.

What to Expect

Will the dentist explain the treatment?

Yes. All treatment options, clinical recommendations, and costs are discussed thoroughly before we begin any procedures.

Can I ask questions?

Absolutely. We encourage patients to discuss their doubts, dental anxiety triggers, or procedural questions with our team.

Can children visit for preventive check-ups?

Yes. Regular check-ups from early childhood help prevent cavities and build positive associations with clinical visits.

Do I need treatment immediately?

Not always. Dental procedures depend entirely on active diagnosis. Many visits are purely preventive or consultative.
