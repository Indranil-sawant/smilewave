Dental Implants

A stable, long-term clinical solution to restore missing teeth following evaluation.

Overview

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically positioned into the jawbone beneath your gums. This post mimics a tooth root, providing a highly stable anchor for mounting custom replacement dental crowns or bridges. Unlike dentures, implants do not slide or shift during chewing or speaking, supporting natural bone health and speech clarity.

Who May Benefit

Following individual clinical evaluations, dental implants may be recommended for patients who have:

  • A single missing tooth surrounded by healthy bone and gums.

  • Multiple missing teeth requiring bridge anchoring without filing down adjacent teeth.

  • Loose dentures that require anchoring support for better chewing stability.

Our Treatment Approach

At SmileWave, implant treatments are planned and executed with precise diagnostic guidelines:

  • Bone & Nerve Diagnostics

    Digital radiographs and detailed scans verify if there is sufficient jawbone density before any procedure is scheduled.

  • Precision Placement

    The titanium post is surgically positioned under sterile autoclave settings to allow bone integration (osseointegration) over several months.

  • Custom Crown Fitting

    Once integrated, an abutment and custom porcelain crown are secured, matching the exact color and bite alignment of surrounding teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants act like natural roots, providing chewing forces that stimulate and maintain healthy jawbone structure. Because they stand independently, they do not require shaving down adjacent healthy teeth, preserving your surrounding dental structures.
