Pediatric care

Gentle smiles for growing kids.

Every child deserves a positive, anxiety-free dental experience. Our pediatric team builds healthy habits in a warm room that feels closer to play than procedure.

Schedule first visit

Dr. Raviraj Dhumal MDS Pediatric Specialist

A space designed for joy

From the waiting lounge to the treatment chair, every detail is meant to lower fear before we even begin.

Ceiling entertainment

Favourite cartoons during treatment help little patients stay still and relaxed.

Tell-show-do

We explain, demonstrate, then treat — never surprise a child with an instrument.

Pristine environment

Class-B hospital-grade sterilization ensuring complete safety for your child.

Ages we see

Infants, toddlers, school-age children, and teens. The first visit is ideal around the first birthday or first tooth.

What we treat

Cavities, hygiene, sealants, fluoride, sports guards, thumb-sucking guidance, and early orthodontic screening.

Parents stay close

You are welcome in the room. We coach you on language that keeps visits positive at home too.
